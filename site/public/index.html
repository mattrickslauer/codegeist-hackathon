Every decision about a building, pinned to the equipment it governs, enforced on every work order.

Walkdown is an app for Jira and Jira Service Management, built on Atlassian Forge.

“RTU-1 must not be shut down on weekdays between 06:00 and 19:00. It's the only air supply for four floors.”

Someone decided that, for a good reason. Three months later a different shift opens a work order:

“Shut down RTU-1 Tuesday 10:00 for coil cleaning.”

Nobody remembers the decision, and the reason never reached the system where work is planned. Walkdown fixes that.

How it works

1. Record decisions on the building

Each site is linked to the Jira space where its work orders live, and shown as a 3D model you can click. Pick a unit and record what was decided, why, and which actions it guards (“shut down”, “isolate”, “drain”). Equipment with standing decisions shows blue.

2. Every work order is checked

When a work order is created or edited, Walkdown finds the equipment it mentions (whole tags only: RTU-1 never matches RTU-10) and checks it against the site's standing decisions. On a conflict it marks the work order, turns the unit red on the model, and posts a Jira comment that @mentions the person who made the decision.

The check is deterministic and explainable. No AI decides whether something conflicts.

3. Conflicts are resolved on the work order

The Walkdown panel on the work order shows the decision, its reason and the matched equipment, with three choices:

  • Reaffirm: the work is consistent after all. Say why; it's recorded.
  • Supersede: propose a new decision. A site approver must accept it.
  • Withdraw: change the work order. It's re-checked automatically.

A Jira workflow validator stops a conflicting work order from moving to In Progress, and blocked work orders are searchable with walkdownStatus = conflict.

Your own buildings

Start from the bundled sample office building, or upload a LiDAR scan (GLB, for example from Scaniverse on an iPhone or iPad Pro) as a site's 3D model.

Runs on Atlassian

Walkdown runs entirely on Atlassian Forge. It has no external backend and sends your data nowhere outside Atlassian. See the privacy policy for exactly what it stores.

Install

Walkdown is in development for Atlassian Codegeist 2026. The install link will be posted here. The source is public on GitHub.

Walkdown records and surfaces decisions. It does not replace a site's safety, engineering or Management of Change sign-off.
