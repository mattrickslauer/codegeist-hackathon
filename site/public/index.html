How it works

Everything below was recorded in a real Jira site running Walkdown, on a real, anonymised five-storey office building.

1

Record decisions on the building

Click a unit on the 3D model and write down what was decided, why, and which actions it guards: shut down, isolate, drain. Units with standing decisions show blue.

Use the bundled sample building, or upload a LiDAR scan of your own from an iPhone or iPad Pro.

2

Every work order is checked

When a work order is created or edited, Walkdown finds the equipment it names. Whole tags only: RTU-1 never matches RTU-10.

On a conflict it flags the work order and posts a comment that @mentions the person who made the decision, so they hear about it the Jira way.

3

It can't start until someone decides

Add Walkdown's validator to your workflow's start transition and Jira refuses to move a conflicting work order to In Progress.

Blocked work orders are one search away: walkdownStatus = conflict.

4

Resolve it on the work order

Reaffirm if the work fits after all, and say why. Supersede to propose a new decision, which a site approver must accept. Withdraw to change the work; it's re-checked automatically.

Every answer is recorded next to the decision it touched.

The Walkdown agent answers in Rovo chat: what feeds RTU-2, how loaded its panel is, which decisions guard it. It names the source of every number and says when one is illustrative.

Install on Jira
  1. Install Walkdown on your Jira Cloud site.
  2. Open Apps → Walkdown.
  3. Choose Explore the sample building, or create a New site linked to your space.

Walkdown records and surfaces decisions. It does not replace a site's safety, engineering or Management of Change sign-off.
