Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026

This policy covers the Walkdown app for Jira and Jira Service Management (“Walkdown”, “the app”) and this website. Walkdown is made by Mattricks Lauer (“we”, “us”).

The short version

  • Walkdown runs entirely on Atlassian Forge. It has no external servers and makes no network calls outside Atlassian.
  • Everything the app stores is stored by Atlassian, in the app's storage for your Atlassian site, or in your own Jira.
  • The only personal data the app stores is Atlassian account IDs, to record who did what. It does not store names, email addresses or passwords.
  • We don't sell data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.

What the app stores

In Forge app storage (hosted by Atlassian, separate for each site that installs the app):

DataContents
SitesName, description, the linked Jira space, approvers (account IDs), and who created the site and when
DecisionsStatement, reason, equipment tags, guarded actions, status, the originating work order, and who decided and approved it (account IDs) and when
ResolutionsWhen a conflict is reaffirmed: the reason given, who gave it (account ID) and when
Building contextEquipment, its connections and their sources, for each site
3D scansGLB files you upload, with file name, size, who uploaded it (account ID) and when. Stored in Atlassian's Forge Object Store

In your own Jira, using Jira's standard features:

  • issue properties on work orders, holding the check result and the matched equipment
  • a comment on a conflicting work order that @mentions the person who made the decision
  • if an admin runs the equipment import, a Walkdown schema and its objects in Jira Assets

What the app reads

When a work order is created or edited, the app reads its summary, description, project and status, and checks it against decisions if it is in a Jira space linked to a site. It also reads Jira Assets objects in the Walkdown schema. It reads only what it needs for those checks.

Rovo

If your site uses Atlassian Rovo, the Walkdown agent answers questions with the site's equipment and decision data. Those conversations are processed by Atlassian under Atlassian's own terms and policies, not sent to us.

What we can see

We can't read your app storage, scans, decisions or work orders. Atlassian gives us app logs for troubleshooting. They can include account IDs, issue keys, equipment tags and site names, plus error messages. Atlassian keeps these logs for a limited time; we don't copy them anywhere else.

Who can see your data

Anyone on your Atlassian site who can use the app can see its sites, decisions and scans. Comments and issue properties follow your Jira permissions. We share nothing with third parties. Atlassian hosts the data as the Forge platform provider.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Data stays until the app is uninstalled. Deleting a site in the app removes the site record and its building context; uploaded scans stay in app storage until uninstall.
  • When an admin uninstalls Walkdown, Atlassian deletes the app's Forge storage under its Forge data retention rules.
  • Comments, issue properties and Assets objects are part of your Jira and stay there until you remove them.

This website

walkdown.mattrickslauer.com is a static site on Amazon Web Services. It sets no cookies. It loads one first-party analytics script (t.mattrickslauer.com, run by us, not a third party) that counts page views and clicks on the install, support and source links. Outside the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland it keeps a random visitor id in your browser’s local storage; in those regions it asks first, and only keeps an id if you agree. Browsers that send Global Privacy Control are treated as having said no, and adding ?tally=off to any URL stops your browser being counted. It never reads your Atlassian site or the app’s data. Like any web host, AWS may keep standard request logs.

Your rights and questions

To ask about your data, or to have it removed, contact us. Most data lives in your Atlassian site, so a site admin can also remove it directly. We'll post any changes to this policy on this page, with a new effective date.
